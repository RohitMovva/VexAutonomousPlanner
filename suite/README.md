# Path-following test suite

One route per scenario, at the difficulty the competition routes actually demand,
so a tuning change can be judged by which scenarios moved instead of by how one
route felt. Generated by `tools/make_suite.py`; do not hand-edit the route files.

## What the real routes demand (measured 2026-09-12)

| property | the saved competition routes |
|---|---|
| segment length | mean 2.6 ft; most never reach cruise |
| reversals | a reverse cusp at almost every node |
| turning radius at speed | p25 0.54 ft, median 1.0 ft, p75 2.0 ft |
| tightest radii | 0.1–0.3 ft, taken at 3–6 in/s beside the cusps |
| longest straight | 0.7 ft typical, 3.6–4.6 ft on sawp and skills |
| action points | 4–18 per route, fired while moving |
| in-place turns | none |

## The routes

Every route starts at the robot pose `(0.069, -23.664, pi)` — the turn_test start —
except `cruise_8ft`, which starts at `(48, -24, pi)`, and the `slip_*` routes (see below).

| route | geometry | exercises | watch |
|---|---|---|---|
| hop_30in | 30 in straight | the typical segment; stop accuracy at a low peak | final error, creep |
| cruise_8ft | 96 in (4 tile) straight | top-end velocity tracking | saturation, straight wheel gain |
| spin_w1 / w2 / w3 / w4 | 720° CCW, hold, 8 in hop, 720° CW, hold, at 1–4 rad/s (turn-node `max_velocity`) | turn cost against yaw rate (scrub), tracking-wheel offsets | `spin_scrub`, `tracking_wheel_offsets` |
| spin_max | the same, planned at 8 rad/s (CW at 14 rad/s²) so the robot runs flat out | top spin rate and spin acceleration | `spin_scrub` top rate / accel |
| arc_R6 / R12 / R24 | 90° arcs from rest | steady yaw per radius band, effective track width | heading p95, rotation delivered |
| corner_R6 / R12 | 24 in, 90° corner, 24 in | corner entered at speed: ramp-in lag, saturation | saturation run length, peak lag |
| chicane_R12 | two opposite 60° arcs | curvature sign flip, yaw acceleration | yaw-accel tracking, one-sidedness |
| vee_reverse | 36 in out, cusp, 36 in back at 60° | the most common real event | error at the cusp, final error |
| reverse_arc_R12 | arc_R12 driven backwards | negative v through the controller | heading p95 vs arc_R12 |
| crawl_R3 | 180° of 3 in radius at 4 in/s | stiction and quantisation at cusp speeds | low-speed tracking, creep |
| lift_while_driving | hop_30in + intake and lift score at 50% | lift/intake load while moving | delta vs hop_30in |
| wall_reset | 52 in to the wall, front reset at the cusp, 52 in back | distance reset size and benefit | reset correction, final error |
| chain_4seg | four 30 in hops with cusps | error accumulation across segments | final error per segment |
| slip_* | five odometry slip tests | see [Odometry slip tests](#odometry-slip-tests-slip_) | wall truth vs odometry at each hold |

Pairs isolate causes: corner_R6 vs arc_R6 separates ramp-in from steady yaw;
lift_while_driving vs hop_30in isolates the lift; reverse_arc_R12 vs arc_R12
isolates direction handling.

## Workflow

```bash
# 1. regenerate (after editing the generator) and check every route hits its band
src/.venv/bin/python tools/make_suite.py
QT_QPA_PLATFORM=offscreen src/.venv/bin/python -m pytest tests/test_suite_routes.py -q

# 2. profile under the current config.yaml and write the trajectories to the card
#    (they land in routes/suite/ and routes/folders.txt is refreshed for the brain)
src/.venv/bin/python tools/profile_routes.py --suite --export /mnt/sdcard/routes

# 3. (optional) make the routes openable in the planner GUI, under <routes folder>/suite/
src/.venv/bin/python tools/make_suite.py --install
```

Routes live one folder deep on the card and in the planner: `suite/` for these
test routes (plus turn_test) and `archive/` for everything else. A card that still
has loose files at the root is sorted once with
`src/.venv/bin/python tools/organize_routes.py --card` (`--local` does the same
for the planner's routes folder; `--dry-run` previews). Always eject the card
(`tools/sdcard` helper, or the planner's SD card menu) before pulling it: a card
yanked mid-write has left `folders.txt` empty, and the brain then shows no folders.

On the robot, pick the route on the brain touch screen: open the `suite` folder,
tap a row or use the up/down buttons (the back row returns to the folder list;
Rescan re-lists the card after a fresh export; the choice survives a reboot). Run
it three times on the same battery, and import the logs:

```bash
cd ../OverrideAutonomous && tests/.venv/bin/python tests/import_logs.py
cp tests/logs/<MMDDYYYY>-<boot>.txt tests/logs/suite_<route>-<n>.txt
tests/.venv/bin/python -m pytest tests/test_path_following.py -q -k suite_
tests/.venv/bin/python tests/run_analysis.py suite_<route>-<n>.txt
```

`test_path_following.py` picks up every `suite_*.txt` automatically. Record each
scenario's best numbers in `manifest.yaml` under `best:` as they come in, so the
next tuning change is judged against them.

## Odometry slip tests (`slip_*`)

Five routes that ask one question: does odometry drift come from wheel slip along
the direction of travel, or from turning while the speed is changing? (The
2026-10-04 analysis of the skills logs found the drift tracked rotation during speed
changes, with opposite sign for braking and accelerating, while straights barely
drifted.) Each route starts and ends with a hold in which two wall-reset sensors
squarely see two different walls. Comparing wall truth at those holds with what
odometry says measures the drift of that one maneuver. They fire no distance resets.

| route | maneuver | isolates |
|---|---|---|
| slip_straight | 26 in straight at full accel and braking | along-track slip (the control) |
| slip_spin | five 90° in-place turns, L/R alternating, 4 in hops, hold after each | displacement from turning alone |
| slip_turn_steady | 90° left at a constant 26 in/s (R18, ramped curvature) | turning at steady speed |
| slip_turn_brake | 90° left taken entirely while braking from 36 in/s to rest at 2.25 ft/s² | turning while braking (the skills pickup turn) |
| slip_turn_accel | the braking turn mirrored: 90° left from rest while accelerating at 2.25 ft/s² | turning while accelerating (the swing out of the loader) |

The turns ramp their curvature in and out rather than joining a straight to an
arc. The planner caps yaw acceleration, so an abrupt joint would make it brake
hard at the joint and the "steady" turn would not be steady. `manifest.yaml`
records, per route, how much of the rotation happens braking, accelerating or at
steady speed (`rotation_share`), and `tests/test_suite_routes.py` holds them to it:
steady 100%, braking 66%, accelerating 62%.

**Where.** All five run in the robot-frame (-x, -y) quarter, which is the
bottom-left of the planner canvas (planner +y points down). Every hold's pose,
sensor readings and beam landing points are in `manifest.yaml` under `holds`.
Beams on the -x wall stay at y ≥ -47 in, clear of the matchloader the skills logs
put on that wall near y = -56. Clear game pieces from the quarter before running.
If some other field element sits where a beam lands, say so, and the routes get
moved rather than read through it.

**Placing the robot.** Position only needs to be roughly right (±1 in), because
the start hold measures it. Heading has to be square, because odometry takes the
start heading as exact: a 1° error looks like ~0.7 in of drift over these paths.
Square the robot to the tile seams before every run. Every route starts facing
the -x wall. Measurements assume a 15 × 16 in footprint centred on the tracking
centre.

| route | front face to -x wall | left side to -y wall |
|---|---|---|
| slip_straight | 42.8 in | 26.2 in |
| slip_spin | 26.8 in | 32.2 in |
| slip_turn_steady | 44.8 in | 46.8 in |
| slip_turn_brake | 45.2 in | 35.2 in |
| slip_turn_accel | 32.8 in | 47.2 in |

**Running.** Run each route five times on a charged battery, interleaving them
rather than doing all five of one in a row, so the battery and motor temperature
average out. Name the logs `suite_slip_<maneuver>-<n>.txt` as with the other suite
routes. A difference between the brake and accel turns that opposes in sign, with
the steady turn and the spins near zero, confirms the speed-change-during-turn
effect, and its size gives the real numbers. Drift in slip_straight alone means
along-track slip.

## Deliberately left out

Straights beyond 10 ft, and radii under 0.25 ft at speed (they only occur beside
cusps, and the yaw cap slows them anyway). The only in-place turns are the
slip_spin test's; no competition route uses one.
